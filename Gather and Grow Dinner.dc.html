About the Event

The Gather & Grow Dinner (formerly the Farm to Table Dinner) is the premier annual fundraising event benefiting the Green Heart Project. Now in its fourth year, this signature event will be led by Chef Nick Dugan of The Crossing and Chefs Charlie Layton & Taylor Cottrell of Basic Kitchen.

The evening begins with a cocktail hour on the Grand Lawn before moving into an elegant ballroom dining experience, where guests will enjoy a fundraising auction.

Join us in advancing our mission to grow food, grow minds, and grow community throughout Charleston.
